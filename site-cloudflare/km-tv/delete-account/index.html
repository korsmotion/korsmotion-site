KM TV — Request account deletion

Last updated: 6 November 2026

You can request deletion of your KorsMotion account and data associated with KM TV without opening the app or signing in to this website.

  1. Email info@korsmotion.ch from the address used for your KorsMotion account. Use the subject “KM TV — account deletion request”.
  2. State that you request deletion of the account and associated data. If you only want a particular report or KM TV data removed, say so. You do not need to provide a reason.
  3. We will verify account control, clarify the scope and respond with the status and any necessary retention exceptions. Never email a password, token or sign-in code.
Open deletion request email

If the email button does not open your mail app, copy info@korsmotion.ch and the subject above into your email service.

Retention and deletion

Deletion covers eligible account identity (email/name), registered devices and identifiers, sessions, linked error reports/attachments, and account-linked cloud history/profiles where present. The KorsMotion account may serve several products: deleting the whole account can remove access in those products too. Local media files, device-local playlists and profiles are not removed by a server request.

Minimum legal/purchase records may be retained where required by law. A pseudonymous used-trial marker may be retained for abuse prevention for up to 36 months from trial use. We explain the applicable exception and expected processing time when confirming your request. This support request is handled by KorsMotion; this page is not an automatic deletion form. Purchases are not refunded and subscriptions are not cancelled by deleting an account.
