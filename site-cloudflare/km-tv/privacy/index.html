KM TV Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This policy covers KM TV (com.korsmotion.tv), including its KM Player media functions. The controller is Sergej Korsakov, trading as Kors Motion (KorsMotion), Bischofszell, Canton Thurgau, Switzerland. Privacy contact: info@korsmotion.ch. KM TV plays sources selected by you; KorsMotion does not supply IPTV channels or obtain rights to your media.

1. Playlists, media and viewing history

Your IPTV/EPG addresses, provider credentials, favourites, playback positions, viewing history, local profiles and settings are used for the functions you select and are generally stored on your device. Playlist and playback requests go to the provider you choose; that provider receives connection details such as your IP address and any credentials its service requires. Local files are not uploaded to KorsMotion merely because you select or play them. Optional account or cloud functions send the information needed for that function; selecting a cloud source does not make all local files available to KorsMotion.

2. Files, cloud sources and metadata

Access to local media, USB, a home network or cloud storage is used when you select those features and grant the necessary access. Connected providers receive requests, authentication information and file identifiers needed to list or play your chosen content. Metadata or artwork searches can send titles, artists or search terms to TMDB, MusicBrainz/Cover Art Archive or TheAudioDB. Update checks may contact Google Play or GitHub; an explicitly started network test contacts the configured test service. These services process their own connection and request data.

3. Diagnostics and support reports

The app keeps a bounded local technical event history to investigate errors. Pressing Send to Center creates and sends a report, optionally with your comment. It can include your account/device association, installation and session identifiers, model, Android/app version, error codes, recent actions, network type, buffering, loading, renderer and playback-clock measurements. Automatic Center reports are off by default and require the corresponding setting; they can be turned off again. An optional screenshot is sent only when supported and separately selected; current builds may not support attachments.

The app filters provider URLs, credentials, tokens, email addresses and local paths from support evidence. Filtering is a safeguard, not a guarantee that anything you type is anonymous: do not include passwords or private content in comments. Device identifiers and technical reports can be linked to an account and are not anonymous. If Sentry is configured in a distributed build, crash/error diagnostics and technical breadcrumbs may also go to Sentry independently of the Center automatic-report setting; the current 720 build has Sentry remote reporting disabled. Any additional diagnostic integration requiring separate consent is not enabled by the ordinary Center setting.

4. Account, device recognition and purchases

Account and licence functions process your email, account ID, sign-in/session information, registered devices, manufacturer/model, Android/app versions and licence/trial status. Google sign-in, when chosen, involves Google. Device recognition uses a hashed Android app/signing-key-scoped identifier where available, with an installation identifier fallback. A hash is pseudonymous data and can remain stable across reinstallations; it is not a device serial number, MAC address or IMEI. These data support authentication, device limits, licensing and abuse prevention. Credentials are protected on the device. If you use Google Play purchases, Google processes payment details; licence and purchase/verification records may be processed to provide your entitlement. Signing out or unlinking a device does not delete the account.

5. Recipients, security and international processing

KorsMotion uses Cloudflare for hosting, account/diagnostic infrastructure and relevant delivery/storage functions. Google provides Google Play and optional sign-in/payment services. Sentry receives diagnostics only when configured as described above. Your selected IPTV, cloud and metadata providers process the requests needed for their services. Access to support data is restricted to authorised support/administration. We do not sell your personal data.

Account and Center requests use HTTPS. User-supplied IPTV, local-network or other sources can use HTTP, so encryption of every app connection cannot be promised. Service providers may process data outside Switzerland or your country, subject to their locations and applicable safeguards. Requests to this policy website also expose IP/HTTP metadata to its hosting provider; the site uses a short-lived visit counter based on a partial IP address, with a 24-hour expiry. This policy page is publicly available without registration. Hosting services may also process technical delivery and performance measurements.

6. Retention and deletion

Account, licence and device records are kept while needed to operate your account and resolve related requests. Logout and session expiry do not by themselves erase stored records. Center report metadata normally has a 180-day retention threshold. Unresolved investigations or records on an explicit hold can be kept longer until the case is resolved or the hold ends; eligible records are then removed by scheduled cleanup. Attachments normally have a 30-day period, or up to 90 days for active cases, subject to an explicit hold. Locally queued ordinary support reports expire after seven days; manual reports have bounded count/size limits and are removed from the queue after confirmed delivery. These limits do not describe an exported log you chose to keep. Anonymous diagnostics, if enabled for a build/service, are retained for up to 30 days.

Request account deletion using the external deletion page or info@korsmotion.ch. We verify that you control the account and explain the processing status and any necessary retained categories. Deletion covers eligible account identity, device/session associations, linked reports/attachments and any account-linked cloud history/profiles. Local media and device-local playlists/profiles are not erased by a server request. Minimum purchase/legal records may remain where required by law; a pseudonymous used-trial marker may remain for abuse prevention for up to 36 months from trial use. Such retained data is restricted to that purpose. Deletion is not a refund or subscription cancellation; manage subscriptions in Google Play.

7. Your choices, rights and children

You can choose sources, disconnect optional services, clear local app data, disable automatic Center reporting and request access, correction or deletion via the privacy contact. Where processing relies on consent, you can withdraw it for future processing. Where applicable, you can object, restrict processing, request portability or complain to the competent data-protection authority, including the Swiss FDPIC. We may need proportionate identity verification and will explain any applicable exception.

KM TV is not directed at children and does not knowingly seek personal information from children. A parent or guardian can contact us about a child account or report. We update this policy when relevant processing changes and show the revision date; material changes requiring renewed consent are not authorised merely by publishing a revised page.
